Camera Permission Setup

To use the camera feature in Screen Recorder, we need your permission to access the camera. Click the button below and allow camera access when prompted.

What to do:
  1. Click "Request Camera Permission" button above
  2. When browser asks, click "Allow"
  3. You should see your camera preview
  4. Close this tab and use the extension!
